Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026 · Version 7

This policy explains what information the mobile game Sand Fit: Block Puzzle (the “Game”) collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The Game is published by Ömer Faruk Gürün, using the name Gurun Studio (Türkiye), referred to as “we”. Contact: 1omerfarukgurun@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • The Game has no sign-in screen. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress, coins, boosters and purchases are stored on your device and backed up to a cloud save under a random, pseudonymous player ID. Restoration depends on the platform and available credentials and is not guaranteed. Settings stay on your device.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. Versions containing the Unity Ads SDK may also use Unity Ads through AdMob mediation when configured. Some earlier releases contain the AppLovin SDK; including a network SDK does not mean that its ads are enabled. Google Firebase provides cloud saves, analytics and crash reports.
  • The Game uses the Meta SDK to measure our Instagram and Facebook ads. Its activation is controlled by the Google consent status. Older releases enable automatic app-event logging, which can include purchase events in addition to installs and launches.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.

3. Advertising, notifications and choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google’s consent form, which also lists Google’s advertising partners. If you do not consent, ads may be non-personalised, limited, or unavailable, depending on the consent state and Google's requirements. In versions that display “Ad privacy choices” in Settings, that button reopens Google's privacy-options form when required. Earlier versions do not include that button; device-level controls and the contact address remain available.

iOS: the Game asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). AdMob, Meta and Unity Ads in supported versions may access your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow it. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).

Personalised ads involve automated profiling by the advertising providers listed above based on the advertising data described above. We do not make any decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects. Buying Remove Ads removes the ads shown between levels; ads you choose to watch for rewards remain available.

Local reminders: with your device permission where required, the Game schedules notifications on the device. It stores recent play times, last-session time and permission-prompt history locally. It does not use Firebase Cloud Messaging or a remote push-token service. Notification permission/opening events, reward-calendar use, rescue results and thermal-state events may be included in Firebase Analytics. You can disable notifications in your device settings.

Reading is not consent: accepting the Terms or acknowledging this notice does not itself grant advertising, analytics or tracking consent. ATT concerns cross-app tracking; Google UMP concerns supported advertising privacy messages. Neither is a blanket consent for every SDK. Released versions currently have no separate in-game Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics opt-out; contact us about your rights and choices.

7. Retention

  • Progress and settings stay on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data.
  • The cloud save and authentication record are retained to provide restoration until their deletion is requested or the service is ended. The current implementation does not automatically delete inactive saves after 24 months.
  • User/event-level Analytics data follows the Google Analytics property's configured retention period. In the standard service this is generally 2 or 14 months; new activity can reset user-level retention. Aggregated reports are not subject to the same retention limit.
  • Firebase states that removal of crash reports and associated identifiers from live/backup systems begins after 90 days; this is not a promise of complete erasure on day 90.
  • Support emails are kept as long as needed to handle your request.
  • The Game keeps recent delivery transaction IDs locally to avoid duplicate rewards. Store and Unity records follow their applicable retention terms; legally required records may be retained where necessary. Advertising and SDK service records follow the relevant provider's retention and deletion rules.

Requests cover the cloud-save document and associated Firebase Authentication record, not merely the local app. Provider backup deletion may take longer under their published schedules. Deletion cannot be undone.

Additional identifiers or provider-specific procedures may be needed. We do not promise that a device model and installation date uniquely identify every SDK record.
