Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026 · Version 2

This policy explains what information the mobile game Sand Fit: Block Puzzle (the “Game”) collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The Game is published by Gurun Studio (Ömer Faruk Gürün, Türkiye), referred to as “we”. Contact: 1omerfarukgurun@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • The Game has no sign-in screen. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress, coins, boosters and purchases are stored on your device and backed up to a cloud save under a random, anonymous player ID, so they can come back if you reinstall the Game. Settings stay only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob and uses Google Firebase for the cloud save, analytics and crash reports.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.

3. Advertising and consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google’s consent form, which also lists Google’s advertising partners. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads.

iOS: the Game asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). Your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) is used by AdMob only if you allow it. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).

Personalised ads involve automated profiling by Google based on the advertising data described above. We do not make any decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects. Buying Remove Ads removes the ads shown between levels; ads you choose to watch for rewards remain available.

7. Retention

  • Progress and settings stay on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data.
  • The cloud save is kept until you ask us to delete it, or until it has not been used for 24 months.
  • Analytics data is kept according to the retention setting of our Google Analytics property (at most 14 months for user-level data).
  • Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.
  • Support emails are kept as long as needed to handle your request.
  • Records of purchases are kept for as long as tax and consumer law require.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Delete your data: uninstalling the Game (or clearing its data in your device settings) deletes the progress and settings on your device. To also delete the cloud save, email us the Player ID shown at the bottom of the Game’s settings window; we delete it within 30 days. Neither can be undone.
  • Other requests: email us. Depending on where you live, you have the right to:
    • know whether we process your data, and get access to it and a copy of it;
    • have inaccurate data corrected, and have your data deleted;
    • restrict or object to processing, including processing based on our legitimate interests;
    • receive your data in a portable format (data portability);
    • withdraw your consent at any time;
    • know the purposes of processing and the third parties, in Türkiye or abroad, to whom your data is transferred, and ask that corrections or deletions be passed on to them;
    • object to a result against you that arises only from automated processing;
    • claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.
    These rights include your rights under Article 11 of the KVKK. We answer free of charge within 30 days. Because the Game has no named account, we need your Player ID (for the cloud save) or details such as your device model and the date you installed the Game to find the data a request refers to.
  • Ads: on iOS turn tracking off in iOS Settings; on Android reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • US state privacy rights: we do not sell personal information for money. Personalised advertising may count as “sharing” under some US state laws; you can opt out by turning off tracking (iOS) or deleting your advertising ID (Android), or by emailing us.
  • Complaints: you can complain to your local data protection authority (in Türkiye: the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK; in the EU: the authority of your country).
